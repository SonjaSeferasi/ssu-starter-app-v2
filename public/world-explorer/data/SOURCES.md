# World Explorer country catalog

Reviewed on October 27, 2026. This is a bundled factual snapshot, not a live population or political-leader feed.

- Country scope: exactly the [193 current UN Member States](https://research.un.org/en/unmembers/currentmembers). The [Holy See and State of Palestine are non-member observer states](https://research.un.org/en/unmembers/observers); they are outside this explicitly chosen 193-country collection. This scope is not a claim that other definitions of country are invalid.
- Grouping and ISO identifiers: [United Nations M49 English table](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/methodology/m49/overview/). We divide the Americas into South America and North America, with North America including Northern America, Central America and the Caribbean. Each member appears once. Russia is assigned to Europe; Türkiye, Cyprus, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Georgia and Kazakhstan to Asia. These statistical assignments do not describe the full physical territory of transcontinental countries.
- Counts under this policy: Africa 54, Asia 47, Europe 43, North America 23, South America 12, Oceania 14. Total 193. Antarctica has no countries and is a separate bonus expedition.
- Names, capitals, languages, currencies, representative center coordinates and flag emoji: [mledoze/countries](https://github.com/mledoze/countries), downloaded from `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mledoze/countries/master/countries.json`. Derived catalog data is distributed under the same Open Database License 1.0, retained in `COUNTRIES-LICENSE.txt`; credit Mohammed Le Doze and the contributors. The upstream UN membership flag incorrectly included VAT at review time, so it is **not** the inclusion rule. `un-members.json` is the reviewed explicit whitelist. Names for Türkiye, Côte d’Ivoire, Cabo Verde, Eswatini and North Macedonia use current common forms. Nauru's lack of an official capital, Bern's federal-city status and Jerusalem's disputed status are qualified in descriptions.
- Biomes select representative **art directions** for a stylized level. They are not comprehensive ecological classifications, climate claims or country boundaries. Many countries have several climates and environments. No unverified landmark claim is supplied where no landmark has been reviewed.
- Six reviewed landmarks enrich the United States, France, Brazil, Egypt, Japan and Australia records; each record includes its UNESCO or official monument source. Their descriptions are short factual paraphrases, not copied travel articles. Other country records keep their individual capitals, languages, currencies, coordinates and subregion; no invented landmark fills an empty slot.
- The companion `profiles.json` supplies sourced, named place profiles for **all 193 countries**, plus the Antarctica expedition. The game's landmark label and procedural scenery read that profile rather than filling empty base-catalogue fields with guesses. `discoveries.json` carries the educational notes and source attribution. See [the integration notes](../../../documentation/world-explorer/README.md) for the representation and licence boundaries.
- Currency codes were checked against [SIX, the official ISO 4217 maintenance agency](https://www.six-group.com/en/products-services/financial-information/market-reference-data/data-standards.html), List One XML published September 17, 2026. The source already includes Bulgaria's EUR and Zimbabwe's ZWG. Micronesia's missing upstream currency value was filled as USD. Non-ISO labels KID and TVD were omitted from the currency-code list; Kiribati and Tuvalu retain AUD rather than misrepresenting local coinage as separate ISO currencies. Reported currency lists remain metadata snapshots, not financial or exchange-rate guidance.

Rebuild with `node scripts/world-explorer/build-catalog.cjs` after saving the two downloaded sources to `scripts/world-explorer/countries-source.json` and `scripts/world-explorer/m49-source.html`. Review whitelist changes independently before accepting a new membership snapshot.

## Optional free data API

[World Bank Indicators API](https://datahelpdesk.worldbank.org/knowledgebase/articles/889392-about-the-indicators-api-documentation) supports public queries without authentication. Country metadata endpoint: `https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/JPN?format=json`. This returns capital-city coordinates, **not** country center coordinates. The optional integration never changes UN membership, map positions or the bundled capital list, and all levels remain available if the service fails. Data requests are cached for 24 hours and bounded by a four-second timeout. World Bank metadata is supplemental; coverage includes territories and aggregates, so response identifiers are checked against the local whitelist. See [World Bank data terms](https://www.worldbank.org/en/about/legal/terms-of-use-for-datasets) for dataset attribution and licensing.

REST Countries was checked live: its old `v3.1` route returned a deprecation object, even with HTTP 200. Current [v5](https://restcountries.com/docs) requires a key, and its current terms do not permit assuming permanent offline snapshots. It is therefore **not** used by this game. No API key, paid plan or copied current REST Countries snapshot is required.

### countries.dev supplemental integration

[countries.dev](https://countries.dev/) was separately verified on October 27, 2026. Its public endpoint `https://countries.dev/alpha/JP` returned matching ISO codes and Japan metadata without a key, login or payment. The homepage describes the service as free and based on open data including GeoNames (CC BY 4.0). The game requests only catalog-allowlisted countries, validates both returned ISO codes, caches metadata for 24 hours and uses a four-second timeout. All membership, progress, flags, country centers and curated profile facts stay local. Unversioned population and currency fields are omitted from the optional response; fetching a record today does not establish that every upstream fact was updated today. World Bank and countries.dev requests run concurrently, and either failure preserves the bundled profile.

The country profile API adds a `countryApi` object alongside the existing `live` World Bank object. `countryApi` identifies its provider and retrieval time explicitly. Its `flagPath` always points to the validated local MIT flag collection rather than accepting a remote upstream image URL. [Flag attribution and import details](../flags/README.md) are shipped beside all 193 SVGs.

The [ISO-code endpoint documentation](https://countries.dev/docs/api/alpha) and [authentication documentation](https://countries.dev/docs/authentication) confirm that no key or account is needed. The [rate-limit documentation](https://countries.dev/docs/rate-limits) publishes no per-key quota or billing tier, but does describe an abuse ceiling of roughly 60 requests per second per IP and HTTP 429 responses above it. This is a published service policy, not an uptime guarantee. The homepage's GeoNames CC BY 4.0 credit describes underlying geographic data; it does not replace the separate ODbL catalog and MIT flag notices included here.
