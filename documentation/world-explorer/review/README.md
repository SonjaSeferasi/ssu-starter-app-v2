# Japan visual review — October 28, 2026

These are captures of the actual local production build in Microsoft Edge.
Only the browser chrome and window borders were cropped away. The gameplay
pixels were not repainted, generated, sharpened or otherwise retouched.

- `japan-gameplay-2026-10-28.png`: normal player camera at the start of the course.
- `japan-overview-2026-10-28.png`: the full authored route in the paused overview.

Open `/world-explorer?country=JPN&play=1&review=1` to switch between these cameras.
The standard course contains 16 colliding platforms, two wooden bridges, two
moving ledges, 30 coins, three stars, a checkpoint, a discovery and the finish.
The overview awards no progress or passport result.

Compared with the earlier prototype, this revision adds modeled roof tiles,
brackets, balconies and lattice; fuller cherry blossom canopies; a detailed
student explorer; natural rock PBR maps; foreground framing; staggered valley
terraces and waterfalls; and a warmer timber/blue HUD. All terrain and landmarks
are real geometry. [Local texture licensing](../../../public/world-explorer/materials/LICENSES.md)
records the CC0 material provenance.

The environment is a fictional mountain-garden course inspired by Japan. Its
generic pagodas and torii are not presented as measured historic reconstructions
or a literal route up Mount Fuji. No concept image is used as a level background.

This is an art-review iteration. It does not claim an exact match to the supplied
illustration, photorealism, a complete manual playthrough, real-device mobile
acceptance or a sustained 60 FPS result. The observed desktop preview sampled
around 30 FPS; performance and further art refinement remain work to review.
Other countries keep their preview status until their own country-specific work
and testing are complete. These captures are current development evidence and
do not replace the earlier Sprint 2 screenshots.
